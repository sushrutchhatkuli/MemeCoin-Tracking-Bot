# 🛰️ Aegis-Crypto Command Dashboard

> [!info] How this vault is fed
> Notes in `Signals/` are generated by the scanner at `aegis/scan.mjs`.
> Run `node scan.mjs` from the `aegis` folder to refresh.
> The queries below need the **Dataview** community plugin (Settings → Community plugins → Browse → "Dataview" → Install → Enable).

## 💎 Long-Term Investment Watchlist

Market cap ≥ $1M, liquidity ≥ $100k, age ≥ 24h (confirmed), holders ≥ 1,000.

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link AS "Token",
  confidence_score AS "Score",
  market_cap AS "MCap",
  liquidity AS "Liq",
  holder_count AS "Holders",
  age_hours AS "Age (h)",
  signal_verdict AS "Verdict"
FROM "Signals"
WHERE signal_category = "LONG-TERM GEM"
SORT confidence_score DESC
```

> [!note] Empty is expected for now
> Across 453 post-mortem outcomes, **no token has ever been scanned at ≥$1M
> market cap** — discovery runs on DexScreener's new/boosted feeds, which skew
> small. This tier will stay empty until discovery is widened.

## ⚡ Fast Momentum Scalps

Market cap $15k–$300k, under 24 hours old.

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  file.link AS "Token",
  confidence_score AS "Score",
  market_cap AS "MCap",
  liq_to_mcap_pct AS "Depth",
  buys_sells_5m AS "5m B/S",
  age_hours AS "Age (h)",
  holder_count AS "Holders"
FROM "Signals"
WHERE signal_category = "FAST SCALP" AND signal_verdict != "SCAM/AVOID"
SORT confidence_score DESC
LIMIT 30
```

> [!warning] Measured failure rate in this band: 40%
> Of 389 scanned tokens in the $15k–$300k range, **154 fell to effectively zero**,
> median 1.86 hours from scan. The `$300k–$1M` band failed 3% (1/29) over the
> same period. Position sizing matters more than signal quality here.

## 🔬 Post-Mortem Learning Stats

Outcomes of tokens re-checked 1–6h after they were scanned. This is the scanner
grading its own past calls.

```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID
  post_mortem_verdict AS "Outcome",
  length(rows) AS "Count",
  round(average(rows.confidence_score), 1) AS "Avg Score At Scan",
  round(average(rows.holder_count), 0) AS "Avg Holders At Scan"
FROM "Signals"
WHERE post_mortem_verdict
GROUP BY post_mortem_verdict
SORT length(rows) DESC
```

```dataview
  post_mortem_detail AS "Outcome",
  confidence_score AS "Score At Scan",
  holder_count AS "Holders",
  smart_money_count AS "Whales"

```

> [!note] On "self-learning"
> `aegis/learning_history.json` accumulates labelled outcomes and computes rug
> rates by holder bucket. It does **not** auto-tune the scoring weights — the
> sample sizes are far too small for that to be anything but overfitting. Read
> the aggregates, then change weights deliberately.

## 🧭 Reading the signals

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `signal_verdict` | Mechanical classification: `BUY SIGNAL`, `WATCH`, `CRASH WARNING`, `SCAM/AVOID` |
| `confidence_score` | 0–100 composite of demand, depth, distribution, momentum, traction |
| `security_status` | Zero-tolerance gate. `FAILED` forces `SCAM/AVOID` regardless of price action |
| `liq_to_mcap_pct` | Pool depth vs market cap. Below 15% means severe exit slippage |
| `top_10_holder_pct` | Non-DEX holder concentration. Above 25% means a cabal can dump on you |
| `buys_sells_5m` | Net demand micro-structure. `BUY SIGNAL` requires buys > 2× sells |
| `dev_reputation` | Deployer history. `SERIAL RUGGER 🔴` forces `SCAM/AVOID` even on a clean contract |
| `smart_money_count` | Watchlist wallets among current holders. Zero unless you populate `smart_wallets.json` |
| `holder_density_status` | Unique-holder floor (150). Failing caps the score at 45 regardless of everything else |
| `social_presence` | Declared X/Telegram/website links. Absence is the reliable signal here, not presence |
| `post_mortem_verdict` | `PENDING` until re-checked 1–6h later, then `WIN` / `FAIL` / `NEUTRAL` |
| `blacklisted` | Deployer or mint on the permanent blocklist — score forced to 0 |

### Verdict precedence

Applied top-down; the first match wins:

1. `⛔ blacklisted` → score **0**
2. `🚨 SERIAL RUGGER` → score **0**
3. `UNVERIFIED / LOW HOLDERS` (< 150 holders) → capped at **45**
4. `SCAM/AVOID` (contract audit failed) → capped at **20**
5. `UNVERIFIED` (audit incomplete) → capped at **40**
6. `CRASH WARNING` → capped at **45**
7. `BUY SIGNAL` / `WATCH` → full score

> [!caution] Not financial advice
> Every note here is automated analysis of public on-chain data, not an investment
> recommendation. A `PASSED` security audit means the contract lacks specific known
> rug mechanisms — it does not mean the token is safe or that it will go up. Most
> low-cap tokens go to zero.
